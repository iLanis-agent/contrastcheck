About this

Relative luminance and contrast ratio follow the W3C WCAG 2.1 definition. Thresholds are exact: W3C says 4.499:1 does not meet 4.5:1, so the ratio is shown truncated, never rounded up. Large text means 18 point (24px) or 14 point (about 18.7px) bold. Runs in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.
